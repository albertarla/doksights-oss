# Getting Started with DokSights

DokSights is a powerful platform that transforms your static PDF and web-based manuals into interactive, AI-powered resources. This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get your workspace up and running.

## 🌟 What is DokSights?

DokSights provides a centralized hub for all your product documentation. It allows you to:
- **Centralize Knowledge**: Store and organize all your manuals in one place.
- **Enable AI Conversations**: Let users "chat" with your manuals to find answers instantly.
- **Build a Branded Portal**: Create a public-facing portal for your customers.
- **Gain Actionable Insights**: See which manuals are being read and what your users are asking about.

## 🏁 Your First Steps

### 1. Complete Your Profile
When you first log in, ensure your profile information is correct. You can manage your account settings from the dashboard.

### 2. Configure Your Portal
Head over to the **Portal Settings** to define your unique portal URL (slug) and upload your company logo. You can also pick a primary brand color that will be applied across your public portal.
> [!NOTE]
> Your portal is disabled by default. You can toggle it on once you've uploaded your first manuals.

### 3. Upload Your First Manual
Go to **Manuals Management** and click on "Upload Manual". You can upload a PDF or create a "Web Manual" for structured online content. DokSights will automatically index the content for AI chat.

### 4. Organize with Folders
As your library grows, use **Folders** to group related manuals. This makes it easier for your team and your customers to find what they need.

### 5. Invite Your Team
Don't work alone! Use the **User Management** page to invite colleagues. You can assign them as Admins or Members with specific permissions for analytics, manual management, or portal settings.

## 🗺️ Understanding the Workspace

- **Dashboard**: Your home base. See the status of your portal, quick stats, and browse your manuals.
- **Manuals Management**: The control center for all your content.
- **Analytics**: Real-time data on how your manuals and AI chat are performing.
- **Company Users**: Manage who has access to your workspace.
- **Portal Settings**: Branding and visibility controls.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To enable AI features, your manuals must be indexed. This happens automatically after upload, but you can track the "RAG Status" on the Manuals Management page.
