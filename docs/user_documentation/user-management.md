# User Management & Permissions

DokSights is built for collaboration. You can invite your entire team and control exactly what they can see and do within your workspace.

## 👥 Roles and Access

There are two primary roles within a company:

### 1. Admin
Admins have full control over the workspace, including:
- Managing portal settings and branding.
- Inviting and removing users.
- Managing licenses and monitoring usage.
- Creating, editing, and deleting manuals and folders.
- Viewing all analytics.

### 2. Member
Members are typically responsible for content management. Their access is controlled by individual permissions.

## 🔑 Permissions

You can fine-tune access for each user (especially Members) using the following permissions:

- **Analytics**: Access to the Analytics dashboard and chat insights.
- **Users**: Ability to invite new members and manage existing ones.
- **Portal**: Ability to change branding, logos, and portal visibility.
- **Manuals**: Ability to upload, edit, and organize manuals and folders.

## 📧 Inviting Users

1. Navigate to the **Company Users** page.
2. Click **"Invite User"**.
3. Enter the user's email address.
4. Select their initial permissions.
5. DokSights will send an invitation email with a secure link.
6. Once they click the link and sign up, they will be automatically joined to your company with the pre-assigned permissions.

## 🛠️ Managing Your Team

- **Update Permissions**: You can change a user's permissions or promote them to Admin at any time.
- **Disable/Enable**: Temporarily revoke access by disabling a user.
- **Remove**: Permanently remove a user from your company.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The first person to sign up for a company is automatically assigned the **Admin** role with all permissions enabled.
