# Company Portal & Branding

The Company Portal is your public-facing storefront for documentation. It allows your customers to browse, search, and chat with your manuals in a professional, branded environment.

## 🎨 Branding Your Portal

Make the portal feel like your own by navigating to **Portal Settings**:

- **Company Logo**: Upload your logo (SVG, PNG, or JPG) to be displayed in the portal header.
- **Primary Color**: Select a color that matches your brand. This color will be used for buttons, links, and highlights throughout the portal.
- **Portal URL (Slug)**: Define a unique, URL-safe name for your portal (e.g., `https://your-domain.com/c/your-company`).
- **Portal Title**: Set a descriptive title for the browser tab and SEO.

## 🌐 Visibility Controls

- **Enabled Toggle**: You can turn your entire portal on or off with a single click. When off, visitors will see a "Portal Not Found" page.
- **Manual Visibility**: Remember that only manuals marked as **"Public"** will appear on the portal.

## 🏠 Portal Layout

The portal is designed for ease of use:
- **Hero Section**: Welcomes users with your brand name and a call to action.
- **Discovery Search**: A prominent search bar powered by AI (see [AI Chat & Discovery](ai-chat-discovery.md)).
- **Folder Navigation**: Users can browse manuals organized by the folders you created.
- **Manual Cards**: Show titles, descriptions, and summaries to help users decide what to read.

## 📱 Mobile-First Experience

The DokSights portal is fully responsive. Whether your customers are on a desktop, tablet, or smartphone, they will have a premium experience:
- **Collapsible Sidebars**: The AI chat and discovery panels adapt to screen size.
- **State Preservation**: Users can switch between reading the document and chatting with AI without losing their place or conversation history.
