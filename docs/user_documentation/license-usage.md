# License & AI Token Usage

DokSights is a managed SaaS platform. Your access to features and resources is governed by your company's license and AI usage quotas.

## 📜 Understanding Your License

Your license determines the limits of your workspace:
- **Max Manuals**: The total number of manuals (PDF or Web) you can store.
- **Max Public Manuals**: The number of manuals you can make visible on your public portal.
- **Max Users**: The total number of team members you can invite.
- **Feature Access**: Some advanced features, like detailed Analytics, may be gated behind specific license tiers.

## 🤖 AI Token Quota

Because AI models charge per "token" (roughly a word or part of a word), we monitor token consumption to ensure sustainable usage.

### What Consumes Tokens?
- **Manual Indexing**: When you upload a manual, we use tokens to read and "understand" the content.
- **AI Chat**: Every message sent by a user and every response generated by the AI consumes tokens.
- **Discovery Search**: Using natural language to find manuals also uses a small number of tokens.
- **AI Classification**: Our background analytics pipeline uses tokens to summarize and categorize your chat logs.

### Monitoring Usage
You can view your current monthly usage in the **Superadmin Dashboard** (for platform owners) or via usage alerts in your own workspace.
- **Monthly Reset**: Quotas typically reset at the beginning of each billing cycle.
- **Soft Limits**: You'll receive a warning when you approach your limit.
- **Hard Limits**: Once a hard limit is reached, AI features (Chat, Indexing, Discovery) will be temporarily disabled until the next cycle or a license upgrade.

## 💡 Optimizing Your Usage

- **Curate Your Content**: Only upload manuals that are truly needed.
- **Clear Descriptions**: Good manual descriptions help the Discovery AI find the right content with fewer turns.
- **Monitor Trends**: Use the Analytics dashboard to see which manuals are driving the most chat activity.

---

> [!TIP]
> If you find yourself consistently hitting your limits, contact your account manager to discuss a license upgrade.
