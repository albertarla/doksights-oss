# Analytics & Insights

Understanding how your manuals are used is key to improving your documentation and customer support. DokSights provides a rich analytics dashboard with real-time data and AI-powered insights.

## 📈 Portal Traffic

- **Unique Visitors**: See how many distinct individuals (based on anonymized IP hashes) visited your portal.
- **Manual Views**: Track total views across all manuals over time.
- **Real-Time Data**: The dashboard updates automatically as users interact with your portal.

## 📖 Manual Performance

Identify which manuals are most popular and how users are engaging with them:
- **Top Manuals**: A list of your most viewed manuals.
- **Reading Duration**: (Future Feature) See how much time users spend on each manual.
- **Referrer Data**: Understand where your traffic is coming from.

## 🧠 AI-Powered Chat Insights

This is where DokSights truly shines. We don't just log chat messages; we analyze them:

### 1. Automated Classification
Our background AI pipeline automatically reviews completed chat sessions and categorizes them:
- **Bug**: User reported an issue with a product or the documentation.
- **Missing Info**: User asked for something not found in the manual.
- **Support**: General troubleshooting or "how-to" questions.
- **Feedback**: Positive or negative comments about the content.

### 2. Theme Clustering
DokSights groups similar chat questions into **"Themes"**.
- See what the most common topics are across all your manuals.
- Identify "hot spots" where users are consistently confused.
- Use these insights to update your manuals or improve your product.

### 3. Session Summaries
Instead of reading thousands of logs, you can read AI-generated summaries of each chat session to quickly understand the user's intent and the AI's response.

## 🛡️ Privacy-First Approach

We take privacy seriously. Our analytics are **cookieless**:
- We use a rotating daily salt to hash IP addresses, allowing us to count unique visitors without storing personally identifiable information (PII).
- No intrusive cookie banners are required on your public portal.

---

> [!NOTE]
> Chat sessions are classified automatically after 2 hours of inactivity. This ensures we analyze "completed" conversations.
